🦋 Better Fly Maryland — Native Garden Planner

An interactive web application designed to help Maryland homeowners convert turf grass lawns into thriving, deer-resistant native plant sanctuaries that protect the Chesapeake Bay watershed.

🌟 Features Overview

🌿 1. Maryland Native Garden Planner (index.html)

Pathways Explorer: Interactive database of 30 native Maryland perennials, shrubs, grasses, and vines.

Smart Filtering: Filter plants by target ecosystem goal (Pollinator Powerhouse, Hummingbird Haven, Butterfly Sanctuary), light requirements (Full Sun, Partial Shade, Full Shade), soil moisture (Dry, Average, Wet), and plant layer (Anchor, Filler, Groundcover, Grass, Shrub, Vine).

Continuous Bloom Matrix: Visual succession guide ensuring uninterrupted nectar availability from April through October.

"My Yard Plan" Shopping List: Interactive side drawer to bookmark native plants, estimate garden square footage coverage, and export customized plant lists to your clipboard.

No-Dig Sheet Mulching Guide: Step-by-step instructions for 4x4 pocket garden lawn conversion using cardboard and compost.

Deer & Bunny Defense Table: Diagnostic breakdown identifying chew patterns, physical mesh barriers, and aromatic screen species (Mountain Mints, Wild Bergamot, Spicebush).

Water Station Setup: Guidance on Shallow Puddle Stations for bees and moving water solar bubblers for mosquito control and songbird attraction.

PWA Capable: Mobile-first responsive UI ready to be saved directly to iPhone or Android home screens as an app.

💚 Ecosystem & Conservation Mission

Converting turf lawns to native plants directly impacts the health of the Chesapeake Bay Watershed:

Deep Root Absorption: Native plant roots push 5–15 feet down into heavy Maryland clay, absorbing stormwater and preventing sediment erosion.

Zero Synthetic Fertilizer: Eliminates nitrogen and phosphorus runoff that causes algae blooms and hypoxia in local tributaries.

Host Plant Specificity: Supports specialist host insects (e.g., Monarchs on Milkweed, Spicebush Swallowtails on Spicebush, and Maryland's state butterfly—the Baltimore Checkerspot—on White Turtlehead).
